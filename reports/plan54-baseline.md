## Facts this baseline found that the plan's text does not name

1. **`LegalDecisionRecords.DecisionId` is unique across agencies** (`PayrollDbContext.cs:387`, no `TenantId` in the index). A second
   agency's import of the same decisions would violate it. PAY054 makes the index `(TenantId, DecisionId)`.
2. **A third resolver:** `GetConfigurationByRegimeCodeAsync` (above). P-6 covers it with the other two.
3. **The acceptance seed has no deduction types and no holidays** for its `LAW_50_2004`: a seeded acceptance run has no statutory
   deduction. The identity proof of the interim pack is therefore made against the Demo seed's values (P-5), in a test.
4. **The export's employee share is found by name** («تأمين»): on the Demo seed that is `DED-SOC` and `DED-HEALTH` together (9% of gross).
